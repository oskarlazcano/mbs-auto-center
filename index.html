Know your dash

What That Warning Light Means

Some lights can wait a day. Some mean pull over now. When in doubt, call us.

Check engine

Steady: get it checked soon. Flashing: stop driving and call, it can damage the catalytic converter.

Oil pressure

Pull over and shut the engine off. Driving on low oil pressure can ruin an engine fast.

Engine temperature

Overheating. Pull over, let it cool, and don't open a hot radiator cap.

Battery / charging

Your battery or alternator isn't charging. You may have limited time before it won't start.
